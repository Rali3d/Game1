# Asset credits

The 3D models, textures and animations in this folder are by **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com),
from these packs, all released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**:

- Universal Base Characters
- Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy
- Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2
- Medieval Village MegaKit
- Stylized Nature MegaKit
- Fantasy Props MegaKit
- Farm Animals (animated FBX: cow, horse, sheep, pig, llama, pug)

The Imp and Puglin monsters come from Quaternius's **Bestiary** pack, which is under the Quaternius Asset
License: they can be used in games, but the files can't be redistributed. So they are **not in this
repository**. `tools/build_assets.py` builds them into `assets/bestiary/` (gitignored) from a local copy of
the pack, and without them the game uses bats and wolves in their place.

They were copied and downsized from the original packs by `tools/build_assets.py`. No credit is required,
but it's deserved. Consider supporting Quaternius on Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/quaternius
